# Photo sources

- `kohan-courtyard.jpg` is a resized locally saved photo from the official [Kohan Traditional Hotel gallery](https://kohanhotel.ir/en/gallery-2/). It depicts the hotel's courtyard in Yazd.
- `moshir-courtyard.jpg` is a locally saved photo from the official [Moshir Al-Mamalek Garden Hotel website](https://hotelgardenmoshir.com/en/portfolio/room-2/). It depicts the hotel courtyard in Yazd.
- `narenjestan-courtyard.png` is a locally saved photo from the official [Narenjestan Traditional House website](https://narenjestanhouse.com/about-us-2/). It depicts the Narenjestan guesthouse in Yazd.
- `nartitee-courtyard.jpg`, `nartitee-pomegranate-tree.jpg`, and `nartitee-interior.jpg` are locally saved photos from the official [Nartitee Ecolodge gallery](https://nartitee.ir/gallery/). They depict the courtyard, orchard, and interior at Nartitee in Taft.

The source pages do not state an image license. These photos are included in this local design prototype with visible links to their sources; permission for broader public reuse has not been verified.
